# Problem Statement: HC26-GEDA01

## Track 1: Green Energy & Sustainability
- **Organisation:** Gujarat Energy Development Agency (GEDA)
- **Problem Statement ID:** HC26-GEDA01
- **Problem Statement Title:** Development of an Interactive "Mission LiFE" Citizen Engagement Application
- **Category:** Software
- **Theme / Domain Bucket:** Green Energy & Sustainability

---

### 1. Background / Context
GEDA is driving the Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) movement, which focuses on mobilising individuals to adopt sustainable, climate-friendly daily practices. A critical component of this transition is encouraging everyday citizens to actively participate in energy conservation and to explore renewable alternatives such as rooftop solar.

---

### 2. Problem Description
While general awareness of climate change is high, everyday consumers face significant friction in adopting sustainable energy behaviours. There is a disconnect between macro-level climate goals and micro-level daily habits. Citizens lack personalised, actionable tools to measure their individual carbon footprints, provide real-time energy-reduction suggestions, and visualise the tangible impact of their actions.

---

### 3. The Challenge / Desired Solution
Participants must build an intuitive, highly engaging digital platform (mobile or web) that operationalises the "Mission LiFE" philosophy. The application should serve as a personal sustainability companion, creatively incentivising citizens to adopt greener habits and reduce their energy consumption. The platform should be open-ended enough to enable innovative engagement strategies, ranging from social networking to AI-driven insights.

---

### 4. Expected Core Features (Open to Innovation)
1. **Personalised Footprint Tracker:** A streamlined onboarding flow that calculates a user's baseline energy or carbon footprint and visualises their improvement over time.
2. **Behavioural Nudges & Micro-Tasks:** A system that delivers contextual, actionable alerts (e.g., shifting heavy appliance usage away from peak grid hours) and daily sustainability challenges.
3. **Gamification & Social Impact:** A community-driven mechanism, such as leaderboards, localised community goals, or a points-based reward system that validates and encourages green actions.
4. **Actionable Resource Hub:** A feature that bridges the gap between intent and action, such as an automated calculator for rooftop solar feasibility or a directory for sustainable energy vendors.

---

### 5. Target Beneficiaries
- Residential consumers across Gujarat (UGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL, MGVCL, Torrent Power circles)
- Local urban and rural communities
- State administrators monitoring regional conservation efforts and grid peak management

---

### 6. Suggested Technology Stack (Open to Innovation)
- **Frontend:** Flutter, React Native, React.js, or Next.js
- **Backend:** Node.js (Fastify v5 + TypeScript)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL + Redis 7 (In-memory caching and high-velocity leaderboards/state machines)
- **APIs & Integrations:** OpenWeather / Solar Irradiance APIs, Gujarat DISCOM Webhooks / Peak Alerts, Push Services (FCM)
