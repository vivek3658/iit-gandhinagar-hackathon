Interactive Mascot & Forest Garden Sustainability Engine

UrjaVatika operationalises the Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment) movement for the Gujarat Energy Development Agency (GEDA). By blending behavioural nudges, rooftop solar analytics, and a "Forest"-style garden nurtured by citizen energy savings and our interactive mascot companions (Chirpy the Sparrow & Chiku the Squirrel), we turn abstract climate goals into tangible daily citizen habits.

Urja Udyan Mascot Engine: Chirpy & Chiku (ચકલી & ખિસકોલી)

Inspired by the popular "Forest" app gamification mechanism, each citizen tends a digital botanical garden ("Urja Vatika"). When citizens save energy during Gujarat DISCOM peak grid hours, switch off idle appliances, or log rooftop solar generation, their garden flowers bloom, the sparrow chirps in harmony, and the squirrel gathers solar acorns. Unattended peak consumption leads to dry spells, visually nudging immediate behavioral correction.
