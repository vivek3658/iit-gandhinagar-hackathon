# User Interface & Visual Design Specification (3D Skeuomorphic Eco-Pop UI)

The visual language in your onboarding screens is called a **3D Skeuomorphic Eco-Pop UI**.  
This style moves away from flat, boring designs by blending realistic 3D textures with bright, high-contrast gamification layers.  

Here is the exact breakdown of what makes this UI style look so rich and high-quality:

---

## 1. Organic Claymorphism & Dimensionality

* **The Look:** The shapes feel soft, tactile, and touchable, like smooth clay or molded plastic rather than flat rectangles.
* **The Elements:** Cards use heavy, soft outer drop-shadows combined with inner highlights (bevel effects). This makes menus like the Device Monitoring List look like they physically pop off the background.

---

## 2. Immersive Visual Storytelling (Environmental Progression)

* **The Look:** The background isn’t static; it lives and breathes with the app's core purpose.
* **The Elements:** High-fidelity 3D assets (the solar-roof house, the blooming plant, and the expressive sparrow mascot) react directly to user data. The interface feels less like a corporate form and more like a window into a living 3D mini-world.

---

## 3. Integrated Micro-Copy Bubbles

* **The Look:** Data insights are delivered through contextual speech bubbles anchored directly to the mascot character.
* **The Elements:** Instead of hiding tips inside an alert box, the UI treats the sparrow as a real-time guide that "talks" to the user about their running AC or phantom power draw.

---

## 4. Lucid Glassmorphism & High-Contrast Accents

* **The Look:** Frosted glass panels layer cleanly over lush, vibrant environments without blocking the view.
* **The Elements:** Containers like the Your Impact Progress Card use translucent white backing plates. This keeps text highly readable while letting the bright nature-green accents and gold currency tokens snap directly into focus.

---
