Workflow 3: Rooftop Solar Feasibility & Payback Logic

Decision tree applied by solar.service.ts to calculate sizing, central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidies, and dispatch leads to vetted GEDA vendors. 

flowchart TD
    IN([Citizen Submits Roof Details]) --> CHECK_AREA{Roof Area >= 100 sq ft &
Shadow Free >= 70%?}
    
    CHECK_AREA -- No --> COMM_SOLAR[Recommend Community Solar /
Green Tariff from DISCOM
Plant Virtual Solar Flower]
    
    CHECK_AREA -- Yes --> CALC_CAP[Calculate Max Rooftop Capacity:
kWp = Shadow_Free_Area_sqft / 100]
    
    CALC_CAP --> BILL_MATCH{Compare kWp with
Monthly kWh Consumption}
    
    BILL_MATCH --> OPT_CAP[Determine Optimal Capacity kWp
Typically 1 - 3 kWp for Residential]
    
    OPT_CAP --> SUB_CHECK{Capacity Bracket}
    
    SUB_CHECK -- Up to 2 kWp --> SUB_1[Central Subsidy: ₹30,000 / kW
Max ₹60,000]
    SUB_CHECK -- 3 kWp and Above --> SUB_2[Central Subsidy: ₹78,000 Capped
+ GEDA State Green Incentive]
    
    SUB_1 --> FIN_ROI[Compute Financial ROI & Payback:
- Estimated Cost: ₹55,000 / kW
- Net Cost = Total - Subsidy
- Payback Period = 3.0 to 3.5 Years
- 25-Year Net Profit]
    SUB_2 --> FIN_ROI
    
    FIN_ROI --> CIT_CHOICE{Citizen Requests
Formal Quotation?}
    
    CIT_CHOICE -- Yes --> MATCH_VENDOR[Query GEDA Empanelled Vendors
in User's District with Rating >= 4.5]
    MATCH_VENDOR --> DISPATCH[Dispatch Encrypted Lead via Webhook
Notify Citizen with 3 Vendor Quotes]
    DISPATCH --> REWARD_GARDEN[Unlock 'Golden Solar Blossom' in Urja Vatika]
    
    CIT_CHOICE -- No --> SAVE_REPORT[Save Assessment PDF to Citizen Vault]
🇮🇳 PM Surya Ghar Compliant

Accurately mirrors Government of India benchmark subsidies: ₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW+.

⚡ GEDA Vendor Verification

Only verified contractors empanelled with GEDA receive citizen leads, eliminating fraudulent or unqualified installers.

⚡ Zero-Drop Concurrency

BullMQ chunks notifications into 500-user worker batches, delivering 100,000 pushes across Gujarat within 60 seconds without throttling.

📊 Live GEDA Command Center

Streaming SSE (Server-Sent Events) feeds real-time megawatts shaved per district (e.g. Ahmedabad, Surat) directly to GEDA officers.
