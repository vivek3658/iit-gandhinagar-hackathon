# Objective and problem fit

## Challenge

The project targets GEDA problem statement **HC26-GEDA01**, “Development of an Interactive ‘Mission LiFE’ Citizen Engagement Application.” The challenge asks for an approachable way for people to understand household impacts, take practical sustainability actions, and see community participation.

## Prototype objective

URJA Vatika prototypes that engagement loop through:

1. A Flutter Android citizen experience with Google sign-in and profile setup.
2. Rule-based household footprint estimates and action suggestions.
3. Daily quests, learning content, and a waste-sorting game.
4. A virtual garden/mascot, activity feed, and community progression.
5. Backend and web-operator routes for account and campaign workflows.

These are prototype capabilities, not claims of measured environmental outcomes. Inputs and completed actions are user-reported; footprint and savings figures are estimates and are not independently verified. The app does not establish automatic smart-meter or DISCOM data collection, general-purpose appliance scanning/control, or production-ready scale.
