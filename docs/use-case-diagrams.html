⚡ Peak Demand Response

Citizen receives real-time grid surge alert from their DISCOM and commits to shifting high-wattage AC/washing machine away from 6:30–9:30 PM.

🌱 Zero-Cost Phone Sensors

Phone automatically prompts when battery reaches 100% to unplug charger, and warns when leaving home WiFi about idle fans.

💧 Municipal Water Alarm

Reminds citizen 15 minutes before the daily municipal water window ends to switch off terrace water pumps, preventing tank overflow.
