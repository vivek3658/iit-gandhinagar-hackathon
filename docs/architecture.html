System Architecture: Fastify v5 Modular Monolith

Architectural blueprint for the Gujarat Energy Development Agency (GEDA) Mission LiFE Citizen Engagement Backend, combining real-time gamification state machines (sparrow & squirrel mascot / Urja garden), peak-hour demand response nudges, and high-throughput solar analytics.

1. High-Level System Architecture Mermaid Architecture Flow

The system utilizes a Modular Monolith pattern using Fastify v5 with dedicated feature plugins. High-velocity citizen interactions (garden watering, mascot moods, peak-hour clicks) are processed through an in-memory Redis 7 layer with write-behind persistence to PostgreSQL to maintain extreme concurrency during DISCOM grid peak alerts.

2. Fastify v5 Architectural Conventions & Modular Structure AGENTS.md & Fastify Rules Compliance

In adherence with AGENTS.md and .agent/rules/fastify-backend.md, the codebase is structured around strict separation of concerns, testability, and deterministic runtime schemas:

📁 Application Hierarchy

  • src/app.ts: Builds & exports Fastify instance without listening. Enables isolated testing via app.inject().
  • src/server.ts: Resolves environment via config/env.ts, registers plugins, and initiates HTTP listener.
  • src/plugins/: Encapsulated fastify-plugin wrappers for Database, Redis, Authentication, and Rate Limiting.
  • src/modules/<name>/: Feature-contained modules with isolated schemas, controllers, services, and routes.

🔒 Validation & Error Boundary

  • TypeBox Schemas: JSON Schemas compiled with @fastify/type-provider-typebox for zero-overhead validation and type derivation via Static<T>.
  • Response Filtering: Every route explicitly provides response schemas, automatically stripping sensitive attributes.
  • Sensible Error Handler: Centralized setErrorHandler returning standard shapes: { statusCode, error, message }.
  • Zero Console Logs: Fastify built-in Pino logger with sanitization of passwords and tokens.
3. Urja Udyan Mascot & Forest Garden Engine Core Gamification Mechanism

The gamification model combines the focus/discipline psychology of the Forest app with the cultural resonance of Indian garden wildlife: Chirpy the House Sparrow (an urban biodiversity indicator) and Chiku the Indian Palm Squirrel.

🌱 Garden Plot Lifecycle (Urja Vatika)

Citizens own an interactive multi-cell garden plot. Daily energy-saving challenges (e.g., turning off geysers, 24°C AC setpoint, avoiding power spikes between 6 PM - 10 PM) water specific garden zones:

  • Stage 0: Seedling / Seed (Initial onboarding challenge accepted).
  • Stage 1: Sapling (5 micro-tasks completed within Gujarat peak window).
  • Stage 2: Blooming Solar Flora (Rooftop solar logged or continuous 3-day peak reduction streak).
  • Stage 3: Golden Banyan / Neem Haven (Long-term community milestone achieved).
  • Wilting Warning: Excessive peak hour consumption triggers a drought state, recoverable through 2 restorative micro-actions.

🐿️ Mascot Companion Mood & Dialogue Matrix

The mascot reacts dynamically to the user's Mission LiFE energy score cached in Redis:

  • Thriving (Score > 80): Chirpy sings cheerful melodies; Chiku buries acorns around solar panels. Unlocks voice tips in Gujarati/Hindi/English.
  • Content (Score 50-80): Active foraging, encouraging prompt peak-hour appliance switch-offs.
  • Thirsty / Sleepy (Score < 50): Mascot perches quietly near dry bird bath, delivering proactive behavioural nudges.
  • Restorative Tasks: Mascot presents "One-Click Commitments" (e.g., "Switch fan regulator down by 1 point", "Clean solar panel dust").
